Spatial Dependency Graphs for XR Authoring

We introduce a Spatial Dependency Graph (SDG) for AI-assisted XR scene authoring that explicitly represents support dependencies between objects. The SDG enables authoring operations to propagate coherently across spatially related objects while preserving reversible scene states.

Co-motion Dependent objects follow their support object during movement.
Re-support Dependent objects can be reassigned to another compatible support.
Removal Unsupported objects are removed when no valid support remains.
Reverse Previous coherent scene states can be restored through the timeline.
